# E-Binder — Product context for Impeccable

## The product

E-Binder is a completely free, iPhone-first Progressive Web App for people who collect physical Pokémon trading cards. It preserves real possessions, languages and print variants and recreates physical nine-pocket binder pages as beautiful, swipable digital albums. The core task is: photograph a binder page, recognize the exact cards, confirm uncertain positions, and enjoy one's collection.

## Audience and usage scene

People who collect because cards carry memories, nostalgia, favorite artists, characters and completed sets. Many scan after opening packs or photograph several real binder pages in imperfect bedroom lighting. They use one hand on a phone, often at night. Beginners must understand the app immediately. Expert collectors need exact printings, duplicate copy counts, offline reliability and data integrity.

## Surface modes

- Main app and scanner: **Operate**. The scanned card and its artwork are the hero; the interface gets out of the way.
- Personal binder and collections gallery: **Experience** within an Operate shell. Emotional, physical page-turning, but never slower or harder to use.
- Brand / onboarding: **Persuade**. Sell the feeling of collecting, not generic productivity.

## Brand promise

"Deine Karten. Deine Geschichten." / The joy of collecting, beautifully kept.
Memorable, independent, warm, collectible. A small art-directed object, not a crypto-trading dashboard or generic AI admin panel.

## Existing frustrations — authoritative feedback

The user dislikes the current black/navy/cyan palette, anonymous nine-blue-squares logo and Bricolage Grotesque + monospace combination. Previous orange primary accents were also rejected. Redesign them as a single family, not unrelated color changes. The user wants native iOS interaction, natural swipes, reliable page scans, few confirmation steps and an emotionally resonant lovebrand.

## Keep intact

- Product name E-Binder, privacy-first local data, optional Supabase, free access.
- Five primary mobile destinations (Sammlung, Entdecken, + Karte, Binder, Profil) until user-tested changes justify otherwise.
- Exact 3×3 physical pocket positions, card art shown unaltered, no invented possession.
- Clear accessibility, text contrast, 44px touch targets, motion reduction, offline capability.
- German-first writing, cross-platform Latin / Japanese card metadata.
- Vite + TypeScript + native DOM, no framework rewrite.

## Anti-references

Black-on-cyan developer dashboards; glowing neon buttons; fake holographic rainbow gradients; random star/sparkle emojis; generic purple gradient SaaS identity; nine unmodified squares used as a logo; excessive small uppercase labels and faux-tech monospace; cards nested inside decorative cards; another premium aesthetic that increases scanning friction.

## Brand release gate

Identity directions are **proposals**, not production defaults. Put asset sources and previews in brand/ or public/brand-lab.html. Preserve the live theme, logo, typography and PWA icon until one direction is selected and its font licenses, 16px logo test, contrast, iPhone screen, browser and offline checks have passed.
